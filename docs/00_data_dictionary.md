# Phase 0 data dictionary

ACADEMIC SIMULATION — not actual trades.

Generated by `desk.data.dictionary` from the files in `data/processed/` — do not hand-edit; re-run `.venv/bin/python run_all.py --only P0`. Data retrieved 2026-09-16 (cached under data/raw/; rebuilt offline with DESK_OFFLINE=1).

Headline window: **2022-03-01 → 2022-08-31** (daily files); weekly files use weeks ending 2022-03-04 → 2022-09-02. Panel history starts 2018-01-02.

Flags (CONTRACTS §1.2): **DIRECT** = observed from a named public source; **PROXY** = a real observable standing in for the thing we need, or a transformation of real data; **ASSUMPTION** = a judgement with a stated justification. Indicator and label columns (`*_filled`, `*_src`, notes) carry the weakest flag of the series they qualify, so filtering by flag never promotes a proxy.

**Known gaps and caveats**

- `usdinr_filled`: 3 LME days had no ECB fixing and carry the previous ECB row (2018-05-01, 2019-05-01, 2020-05-01).
- Freight: 756 days (2018-01-02 → 2020-12-23) precede the freight series and are left empty (`freight_src = NO_FREIGHT_DATA`); the headline window is fully covered.
- `freight_filled`: 403 days carry a freight value assessed on an earlier day (point-in-time forward-fill from the Thursday WCI assessment; CONTRACTS §3).
- `usd_rate_3m_filled`: 42 days carry a Treasury bill print from an earlier day (US holiday) or the whole FX row. `rates_src` on panel days: USD:UST13W|INR:OECD_IR3TIB 1264.
- MCX: `PROXY_IMPORT_PARITY` on 1264 days. No `data/manual/mcx_aluminium_*.csv` bhavcopy extract exists (mcxindia.com returned HTTP 403 to scripted requests, docs/research/market_data_notes.md §6.1), so every MCX price is the duty-paid LME import-parity PROXY. A third-party mirror is used only to calibrate/check it.
- MCX expiries: the proxy rule (last calendar day → previous LME day) differs from the MCX-published 2022 expiries (exchange.yaml `mcx_al_expiry_dates_2022`) on 2022-08-30 (proxy 2022-08-31); days-to-expiry carry is off by that many days around those expiries.
- Freight levels in window: USEC_MUN ANCHORED 44 days, EXTRAP_PRE 22 days, SIBLING 60 days; JEA_NSA level is an ASSUMPTION on every day (no citable 2022 Jebel Ali → Nhava Sheva quote).
- Freight alignment has no look-ahead (a day sees only assessments made on or before it), but the weekly freight LEVELS are hindsight-calibrated: the anchors behind Mar–Jun 2022 weeks were published Aug–Sep 2022 (up to ~7 months later; `available_from` in data/interim/freight/points_india_inbound_anchors.csv and the weekly `note`). Do not treat freight as information available on the trade date.
- MCX proxy regime bias: unbiased over the window but above the third-party mirror on every March-2022 Friday (+5 to +11 ₹/kg) and mostly below it May–July (−1 to −8 ₹/kg), which widens the parity swing between the spike and the trough (market_proxy.yaml).
- `inr_rate_3m_pa` uses the same month's OECD average (≤ 1 month look-ahead); forwards are CIP-derived, not observed FBIL/RBI forward premia (PENDING in docs/research/market_data_notes.md).
- Column flags: DIRECT 7, PROXY 14, ASSUMPTION 7 (of 28).
