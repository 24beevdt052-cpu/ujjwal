## F1 in numbers: `var_mcx_beta.csv` (new, Phase 4.1, SENSITIVITY)

`run_var.mcx_beta_samplings` measures the same mirror (through `MarketHistory(mcx_source="mirror")`) over the same
window three ways. The daily figure must reproduce `mcx_basis_risk.csv` to 1e-6, or the stage raises.

| Sampling | Beta (s.e.) | t against 1 | R² | n | Mean GARCH VaR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| engine proxy | 1 | — | — | — | ₹4.27 m (optimistic end) |
| lead/lag (Dimson, parity at t−1, t, t+1) | 0.94 (0.10) | −0.6 | 0.47 | 125 days | not run |
| weekly (W-FRI closes) | 0.75 (0.05) | −4.9 | 0.90 | 26 weeks | **₹8.48 m** (central) |
| daily (P3's test) | 0.45 (0.06) | −8.7 | 0.29 | 125 days | ₹17.25 m (pessimistic end) |

Once closing times are matched, unit beta cannot be rejected. On weekly closes it can, and about a quarter of a parity
move is left open. The Monte Carlo basis factor was already estimated on weekly changes, so its numbers are unchanged.
